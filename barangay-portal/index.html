How It Works

Report and monitor community concerns in three simple steps.

01. Submit

Submit an incident report or public service request through the portal.

02. Review

Barangay personnel review the report and approve incidents before they appear on the public Community Issue Map.

03. Track

Monitor the progress of your report as barangay personnel provide status updates.
